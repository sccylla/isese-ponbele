About Isese Ponbele

Isese Ponbele is a digital knowledge house focused on Yoruba culture, Ifá, Òrìṣà, language, heritage and documented traditional knowledge.

Guide

Our purpose

Our purpose is to make Yoruba cultural knowledge easier to discover, read and study online while preserving Yoruba terminology and acknowledging that traditions vary by town, lineage and community.

Guide

How we handle cultural variation

We avoid presenting one local practice as automatically universal. Where traditions differ across Ọ̀yọ́, Ilé-Ifẹ̀, Ekiti, Ondo, Ìjẹ̀bú, Ẹ̀gbá, the Republic of Benin, diaspora communities or particular lineages, we aim to keep that variation visible.

Guide

Sources and documentation

Reference trails may include UNESCO documentation, academic research, museum collections, historical materials and clearly identified community or practitioner sources. Source quality and the type of claim being made matter.

Guide

Corrections and growth

This knowledge house is designed to grow. Terminology, citations and historical context can be improved as stronger documentation becomes available.
