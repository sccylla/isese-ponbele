Iwúre of the Day

Kí Ọlọ́run fún wa ní ìmọ̀, kí a ṣe Ifá má bà wa, kí gbogbo ohun rere máa jẹ́ wa lójú ọ̀nà. Àṣẹ.

May wisdom guide us, may good fortune walk with us, and may goodness meet us on every road.
