Ilé Òrìṣà • Isese Ponbele

Yoruba sacred power, documented beautifully.

A visual archive of major and regional Òrìṣà, deified ancestors and sacred-power complexes. Each entry combines real cultural photography, sacred geography, oral memory, material culture and selected references. The archive begins with 21 entries and remains intentionally open to the many other Òrìṣà preserved by Yoruba communities.

Àwọn Òrìṣà • Documentary directory

Enter the sacred archive.

Real photographed shrines, staffs, monuments, masks, rivers, groves and festival representations introduce each documentary. The imagery represents documented cultural forms—not invented fantasy portraits.

Archive scope: Yoruba sacred tradition is not a fixed list. Ilé-Ifẹ̀, Ọ̀yọ́, Ìjẹ̀ṣà, Ẹ̀gbá, Ìjẹ̀bú, Ekiti, Ketu, Ondo and other communities preserve local sacred histories, titles and manifestations. Ìyámi Àjẹ́ / Àwọn Ìyá is included among the sacred powers here, while classification can differ by lineage and scholarship.
How this archive reads tradition

Visual culture with context.

The archive treats Òrìṣà as living Yoruba traditions embedded in place, family, priesthood, oral literature and material culture—not as a collection of fantasy characters.
