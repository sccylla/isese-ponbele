Spiritual authority, command, power to make things happen; also used as an affirmation.

Ayanmo

Destiny; a person’s chosen or appointed life direction.

A male Ifa priest or diviner trained in the Ifa corpus and divination system.

Ewe

Leaf or leaves; also used broadly in the context of herbal material.

Ifa

The Yoruba divination and knowledge system associated with Orunmila.

Sacred palm nuts used in Ifa divination.

Portion, lot or allotted share; often discussed in relation to destiny.

Goodness, blessing, favourable condition or positive fortune.

Medicine, remedy, preparation or specialised traditional working depending on context.

Divination chain used by trained Ifa practitioners.
