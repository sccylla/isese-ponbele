Ase

Spiritual authority, power, command or the force by which words and actions take effect.

Ayanmo

Destiny; what is understood as the course or portion appointed to a person.

Babalawo

An Ifa priest and diviner trained in the corpus and practice of Ifa.

Ewe

Leaf or leaves; a core term in Yoruba plant and traditional medicine vocabulary.

Ifa

The Yoruba divination and knowledge system associated with Orunmila.

Ikin

Sacred palm nuts used in Ifa divination.

Ipin

Portion, share or destiny; often discussed alongside Ori and Ayanmo.

Ire

Goodness, blessing, favourable condition or good fortune.

Isegun

Victory, overcoming or triumph over difficulty or opposition.

Odu

A sign, chapter or corpus division within Ifa divination and literature.

Ogun / Oogun

Depending on spelling and context, may refer to medicine, preparation or charm; it should be distinguished carefully from Ogun the Orisa.

Opele

The divining chain used in Ifa consultation.

Opon Ifa

The divination tray used in Ifa practice.

Ori

Literally head; also a major Yoruba concept concerning inner head, personal destiny and spiritual individuality.

Orisa

Divine beings or sacred powers within Yoruba religious tradition.

Yoruba

The people, language and broad cultural tradition originating primarily in southwestern Nigeria and neighbouring regions.

Idaabobo

Protection, safeguarding or defence.

Anu

Mercy, compassion or favour.

Owo

Money, wealth or hand, depending on context.

Ile

House, home, land or lineage context, depending on usage.
