What Is Ìṣẹ̀ṣe (Isese)?

Ìṣẹ̀ṣe is a widely used Yoruba term for indigenous tradition and ancestral ways, including religious, ethical, cultural and communal practices. Its expression varies by town, lineage and community.

Guide

Meaning of Ìṣẹ̀ṣe

Ìṣẹ̀ṣe is commonly used today for Yoruba indigenous tradition, especially the religious and cultural systems rooted in Yoruba communities of West Africa. English searches often spell the word Isese, while Yoruba orthography writes Ìṣẹ̀ṣe.

It is useful to think of Ìṣẹ̀ṣe as a living field of inherited practices rather than a single centralized institution. Beliefs, ritual forms, praise language and community customs may differ across Ilé-Ifẹ̀, Ọ̀yọ́, Ẹ̀gbá, Ìjẹ̀bú, Ekiti, Ondo and other Yoruba regions.

Guide

Ìṣẹ̀ṣe, Ifá and Òrìṣà

Ifá and Òrìṣà traditions belong within the wider landscape of Yoruba indigenous religion and culture, but the terms are not interchangeable. Ifá is a divinatory and literary knowledge system associated with Òrúnmìlà, while Òrìṣà refers to sacred powers or divinities with distinct histories, communities and forms of devotion.

The relationship among Ifá, particular Òrìṣà cults, ancestral practices and local institutions can vary substantially by community.

Guide

Character, destiny and communal life

Yoruba religious thought frequently connects spiritual life with conduct, destiny, relationship and responsibility. Ideas such as ìwà (character), orí (the personal head or destiny-bearing principle in many interpretations), reciprocity and respect for elders or ancestors appear across many bodies of Yoruba thought.

These ideas are best understood in context rather than reduced to simple one-line definitions.

Guide

A living tradition, not a museum object

Ìṣẹ̀ṣe continues to be practiced in Nigeria, Benin, Togo and throughout the African diaspora. Contemporary practitioners live within modern cities, professions and technologies while also maintaining inherited ritual, language, festivals and family traditions.

Because Yoruba traditions are diverse, this site documents regional and lineage variation instead of presenting every practice as universal.
